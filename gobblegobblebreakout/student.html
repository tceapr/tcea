Turkey Names

Hens

Female turkeys are called hens. They usually make softer chirping sounds instead of gobbling.

Toms

Male turkeys are called toms. Toms are known for gobbling.

Poults

A baby turkey is called a poult.

Flocks

A group of wild turkeys is called a flock.
