Teacher Directions
  1. Choose a game from the drop-down menu.
  2. Play it together as a class, or copy the game link and send it to your students.
  3. Click Answers to see the answer key, including the four groups and the four items in each group.
For Teachers Only

This teacher tool and password are for your use only. Please do not share them with students. Students only need the link to the specific GroupUp activity you want them to play.

Game Play

Find groups of four items that share something in common.

  1. Select four items.
  2. Tap Submit to check your guess.
  3. Find all four groups before making 4 mistakes.
