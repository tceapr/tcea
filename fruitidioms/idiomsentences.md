

Fruit Idiom Sentence Pick

Directions: Read the idiom. Then read both sentences. Choose the sentence that uses the idiom correctly.

1. In a pickle

A. Nina was in a pickle because everything went perfectly and she had no problems at all.  
**Wrong**

B. When Nina forgot her lunch and her homework on the same day, she was in a pickle.  
**Right**

2. The cherry on top

A. The class party was fun, and the extra recess afterward was the cherry on top.  
**Right**

B. The class party was canceled, and the broken projector was the cherry on top.  
**Wrong**

3. Heard it through the grapevine

A. I heard it through the grapevine that the school play auditions are next week.  
**Right**

B. I heard it through the grapevine when the principal announced it on the morning announcements.  
**Wrong**

4. Peachy keen

A. Ava said everything was peachy keen as she frowned, stomped her feet, and complained about her terrible day.  
**Wrong**

B. After finishing her project early, Ava said, “Everything is peachy keen\!”  
**Right**

5. Compare apples to oranges

A. Comparing a bicycle to a backpack is like comparing apples to oranges.  
**Right**

B. Comparing two red apples from the same basket is like comparing apples to oranges.  
**Wrong**

6. Plum forgot

A. I plum forgot to bring my library book, even though it was sitting by the door.  
**Right**

B. I plum forgot my library book because I remembered it and packed it carefully.  
**Wrong**

7. Go bananas

A. The crowd went bananas by sitting silently and reading quietly.  
**Wrong**

B. The crowd went bananas when the team scored the winning point.  
**Right**

8. Sour grapes

A. When Liam won first place, he smiled and thanked the judges. That was sour grapes.  
**Wrong**

B. When Liam did not win the art contest, he said the prize was boring anyway. That was sour grapes.  
**Right**

9. Cool as a cucumber

A. Even during the spelling bee finals, Mia was cool as a cucumber.  
**Right**

B. Jayden was cool as a cucumber while he screamed and knocked over his chair.  
**Wrong**

10. A lemon

A. My new scooter worked perfectly every day, so it was a lemon.  
**Wrong**

B. My new scooter broke three times in one week. It turned out to be a lemon.  
**Right**

11. Cherry-pick

A. Maya cherry-picked only the facts that supported her opinion and ignored the rest.  
**Right**

B. Maya cherry-picked by eating every cherry in the bowl.  
**Wrong**

12. Plum tuckered out

A. After taking a long nap, Noah was plum tuckered out and full of energy.  
**Wrong**

B. After running laps in PE, Noah was plum tuckered out and needed a rest.  
**Right**

13. In the limelight

A. Sofia was in the limelight when she hid behind the curtain so no one could see her.  
**Wrong**

B. During the talent show, Sofia was in the limelight when everyone watched her solo.  
**Right**

14. The apple of my eye

A. My little brother is the apple of my eye because he means so much to me.  
**Right**

B. My sandwich is the apple of my eye because it fell on the floor.  
**Wrong**

15. When life gives you lemons, make lemonade

A. When the field trip was canceled, our class gave up and refused to do anything fun. We made lemonade out of lemons.  
**Wrong**

B. When the field trip was canceled, our class made the best of it by having a museum day at school. We made lemonade out of lemons.  
**Right**
