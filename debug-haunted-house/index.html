Debug the Haunted House

Welcome to the Haunted House Debugging Challenge!

The friendly haunted house is full of bugs. The doors will not open, the witch cannot reach the moon, the potion recipe is scrambled, and the pumpkins are being carved in the wrong order.

Complete the six debugging challenges before midnight. Each correct answer will give you one letter. Put the letters together to unlock the final message and help the Halloween characters get ready for trick-or-treaters.

Teacher Guide

Enter the teacher password to view the guide and answer key.

Recommended Grade Levels

Grades 3-5

Learning Objectives

Students practice debugging, sequencing, algorithm development, pattern recognition, directional language, spatial reasoning, if-then conditions, and testing instructions.

Time

About 20 to 30 minutes.

Classroom Use

Use individually, with partners, or as a whole-class projected activity. Partners can take turns as driver and checker.

Answer Key
  1. The Crooked Path: change the third arrow from Down to Up. Letter T.
  2. The Potion Room: put the recipe steps in order and move "Add three more spider rings" to Do Not Use. Letter R.
  3. The Witch's Tower: change command 4 from Turn right to Turn left. Letter E.
  4. The Pumpkin Workshop: arrange the safe carving steps in order. Letter A.
  5. The Ghostly Hallway: replace the sixth item, Cat, with Ghost. Letter T.
  6. The Escape Hall: choose "If you see a ghost, turn left." Letter S.
  7. Final word: TREATS.
Discussion Questions
  1. What is a bug?
  2. How did testing help you find mistakes?
  3. Which challenge was most difficult?
  4. Where do people debug instructions outside of computer programming?
  5. How could you make one of the algorithms shorter or clearer?
Extension Activity

Create your own Halloween algorithm with one intentional bug. Trade with a partner and see whether your partner can identify and fix the mistake.
